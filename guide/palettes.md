# Palettes

Quantize and Dither take their colors from a shared **palette**, or from a **generated** palette of 2–8192 colors
that the stage rebuilds automatically from its own input.

Shared palettes live in the Palette panel. You can:

- **Generate** one from the image with median cut, Wu, octree or k-means. Wu and octree reach the requested color
  count even on smooth gradients.
- **Start from a built-in**: PICO-8, NES, Game Boy, CGA, C64 and more.
- **Import and export** `.hex`, `.gpl`, `.pal`, `.act` and `.ase` files (**File › Import Palette…**).
- **Edit** single colors and **lock** the ones you want to keep.
- **Pick** colors from the image with the eyedropper: the **Pick** button, or Alt+click in the viewer. Some Linux
  desktops reserve Alt+click for moving windows; use **Pick** there.
