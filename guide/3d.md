# 3D view and baking

## Opening a model

**File › Open Model…** (Ctrl+Shift+O), or drop a **glTF, GLB, FBX or OBJ** file on the window.

The textures its materials use open with it, each in a tab, whether they sit next to the file, in a common
textures folder or inside a GLB/FBX; a texture already open is used instead. Each material is drawn with its own
texture ([several textures](textures.md#models-with-several-materials)); a model with one material shows the
texture you're on.

## 3D view

The **3D view** shows the processed texture on the model as you work. Its **look** dropdown (also View › 3D Look)
picks how the model is drawn:

- **Lit**: key, fill and rim lights, soft shadows and highlights; reads the AO, roughness and metallic maps
- **Unlit**: the texture's exact colors
- **Wireframe** and **Unlit + Wireframe**: triangle edges alone, or over the texture
- **Clay**: the shape alone, in gray
- **Normals**: surface directions as colors
- **PSX**: 240 lines, vertex wobble, affine texture warping, hard texels, 15-bit color with the PSX dither
- **N64**: 240 lines, 3-point texture filtering, 16-bit color with the N64 dither, smooth edges and a soft picture

**Style…** opens every setting of the look: surface, lighting (off, per vertex or per pixel), wireframe, texture
filter, resolution, upscaling, color depth and dither, ambient, specular, fog, vertex snapping, affine textures,
antialiasing, back faces, shadows and maps. Changing one makes the look your **Custom** style, which stays in the
dropdown until you edit a look again.

Drag to orbit, right-drag (or Shift-drag) to pan, scroll to zoom, **Alt-drag** left and right to turn the sun,
double-click to frame the model; the bottom of the view lists these.

With a texture open but no model, the 3D view shows it on a **shape**: cube, plane, sphere, sphere with the texture
tiled twice, or torus (picked in its toolbar).

The toolbar's **2D · 2D / 3D · 3D** switch (also in the View menu, Ctrl+Shift+1/2/3) shows the texture, the texture
and the model side by side (drag the bar between them to resize, double-click it to even them), or the model alone.
Opening a model while only the texture shows switches to 2D / 3D.

## Live reload

Save the open texture, its map files or the model from another app (Photoshop, Blender, …) and they reload on their
own, keeping the stack, the zoom and the 3D camera. A re-exported model keeps its textures and UV set.
**File › Reload Changed Files** (also in Settings › General) picks what reloads: **All Files**, **Per File** or
**Off**. With **Per File**, each texture in the Textures panel (and the model) has a **Reload on change** switch; the
switches are saved in the project.

## Map baking

The **Bake** panel bakes **AO, cavity, curvature, edge, thickness, height and up-facing** maps from the model's
shape. They are ray traced on the GPU in the model's UV space, with edge padding, at up to 2048×2048. Maps sharpen
while they bake, and you can stop early.

**Preset** at the top of the settings picks a starting point: Quick preview, Balanced (the default), Final quality,
Hard surface, Organic, Weathering or Open meshes. **Save as…** keeps your own settings under a name.

Baked maps land straight in the map slots, so they drive [masks](masks-and-maps.md) and Adjust's shading like
imported ones. They stay when you open another texture (they belong to the model's UVs) and are cleared when you
open another model. **Save…** in the Maps panel writes them as PNGs named so they load with the texture next time
(`rock_ao.png`).
