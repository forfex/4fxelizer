# 3D view and baking

## Opening a model

**File › Open Model…** (Ctrl+Shift+O), or drop a **glTF, GLB, FBX or OBJ** file on the window.

The model's base color texture opens with it, whether it sits next to the file, in a common textures folder or
inside a GLB/FBX. If the texture you already have open belongs to the model, it stays open.

## 3D view

The **3D view** shows the processed texture on the model as you work. Each PSX quirk is a switch of its own:

- vertex snapping (the wobble)
- affine texture warping
- nearest texels
- a 240- or 480-line framebuffer
- lighting
- 15-bit dither

The toolbar's **2D · 2D / 3D · 3D** switch (also in the View menu, Ctrl+Shift+1/2/3) shows the texture, the texture
and the model side by side (drag the bar between them to resize, double-click it to even them), or the model alone.
Opening a model while only the texture shows switches to 2D / 3D.

## Live reload

Save the open texture, its map files or the model from another app (Photoshop, Blender, …) and they reload on their
own, keeping the stack, the zoom and the 3D camera. A re-exported model keeps its texture set and UV set.
**File › Reload Changed Files** turns this off.

## Map baking

The **Bake** panel bakes **AO, cavity, curvature, edge, thickness, height and up-facing** maps from the model's
shape. They are ray traced on the GPU in the model's UV space, with edge padding, at up to 2048×2048. Maps sharpen
while they bake, and you can stop early.

**Preset** at the top of the settings picks a starting point: Quick preview, Balanced (the default), Final quality,
Hard surface, Organic, Weathering or Open meshes. **Save as…** keeps your own settings under a name.

Baked maps land straight in the map slots, so they drive [masks](masks-and-maps.md) and Adjust's shading like
imported ones. They stay when you open another texture (they belong to the model's UVs) and are cleared when you
open another model. **Save…** in the Maps panel writes them as PNGs named so they load with the texture next time
(`rock_ao.png`).
